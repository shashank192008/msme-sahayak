## Brand & Style

The design system projects unyielding institutional trust, financial authority, and modern digital momentum. Designed specifically for Indian Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs)—ranging from tier-1 exporters to tier-2/3 manufacturing and retail proprietors—the interface marries the sobriety of formal banking systems with the intuitive clarity of premier consumer fintech.

The design movement combines **Corporate Modern** with **Tactile Precision**:
- **Tone:** Authoritative, empowering, clear, and reassuring. It resolves anxiety surrounding delayed invoices, working capital crunches, and bureaucratic scheme access.
- **Visual Weight:** Solid, high-legibility surfaces over airy, hyper-skeletal wires. Information density is calibrated to allow rapid scanning of invoice lists, ledger health, and statutory compliance status without cognitive overload.
- **Cultural Resonance:** Dignified and contemporary. It subtly respects Indian financial traditions through deliberate saffron-amber accents and prominent Indian Rupee (₹) symbol hierarchy without resorting to folkloric clichés.

## Colors

The palette balances institutional depth with crisp digital operational signals. Light mode is the default, ensuring high legibility under varying field conditions (from outdoor wholesale markets to dimly lit shop-floor back offices).

- **Primary (`#0F2942` - Deep Navy):** Represents stability, governance, and institutional backing. Used for primary navigation, critical headers, primary actions, and brand foundations.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488` - Modern Teal):** Signifies liquidity, capital growth, and verified digital workflows. Used for operational CTAs, confirmed states, progress indicators, and active workflow steps.
- **Tertiary (`#EA580C` - Deep Saffron / Amber):** Strategic accent used sparingly for pending bottlenecks, action-required notices, regulatory deadlines, and high-priority statutory schemes.
- **Neutral Base:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) cards resting on a Slate canvas (`#F8FAFC`), bordered and articulated by Slate tints (`#E2E8F0`, `#CBD5E1`) with `#64748B` anchoring secondary details and metadata.

### Financial Status Tokens
- **Paid / Cleared:** Forest Emerald (`#059669` text, `#ECFDF5` background).
- **Pending / In Verification:** Warm Amber (`#D97706` text, `#FFFBEB` background).
- **Partially Paid:** Cerulean Indigo (`#2563EB` text, `#EFF6FF` background).
- **Overdue / Alert:** Crimson Rose (`#DC2626` text, `#FEF2F2` background).

## Typography

Typography prioritizes tabular clarity and high readability across desktop monitors and entry-level mobile devices:

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its modern, confident geometry and approachable rounded terminals. It injects energy into dashboard overviews, metric totals, and section titles without compromising executive seriousness.
- **Body & Numerical Values (Inter):** Chosen for its exceptional tabular numbers, micro-legibility, and extensive character support. Inter guarantees that dense invoice tables, GSTIN fields, PAN identifiers, and rupee balances remain legible without glyph distortion.
- **Currency Presentation:** The Indian Rupee symbol (`₹`) is explicitly styled to match the weight of accompanying integers, never trailing or shrinking arbitrarily. High-value amounts use standard Indian numerical group formatting (e.g., ₹12,45,000.00).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts a disciplined **Fluid Grid** structure anchored by an 8-point baseline grid:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** A 12-column grid featuring `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Accommodates split views: primary operational ledger (8 columns) alongside statutory summaries, working capital health gauges, and instant action rails (4 columns).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** An 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Metric cards reflow into 2x2 formations; navigation shifts to a compact rail.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** A 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Touch targets adhere strictly to a minimum 48x48px safe zone. Bottom-sheet modals replace nested desktop dialogs for invoice reconciliation and loan applications.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp, structured separation rather than deep dramatic drop-shadows, creating an enterprise feel:

1. **Base Layer (Level 0):** Background canvas in slate-tinted white (`#F8FAFC`).
2. **Surface Layer (Level 1):** Primary operational cards, invoice logs, and summary containers in `#FFFFFF`. Outlined by a razor-thin border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) paired with an ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.03)`.
3. **Elevated Containers (Level 2):** Hover states, interactive metric tiles, and floating filter chips: `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.05)`.
4. **Overlay Planes (Level 3):** Global search, notification drawers, bottom action sheets, and modal verification screens: `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.08)`. Outlines shift to `#CBD5E1` for unmistakable separation.

## Shapes

The design system implements a controlled **Rounded (`roundedness: 2`)** geometry. 

- **Containers & Data Surfaces:** Default corners at `0.5rem` (8px) provide an engineered, reliable feel while avoiding harsh industrial edges.
- **Metric Banners & Modals (`rounded-lg`):** Utilize `1rem` (16px) radius to softly delineate analytical groupings and application modals.
- **Action Elements & Status Badges:** Status pills, filter tags, and quick-filter chips adopt full pill geometry (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish communicative indicators from structural rectangular surfaces.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#0F2942` with crisp white text, `0.5rem` border radius, and subtle inset top-highlight. On hover, shifts to `#1E3A8A`. Focused via high-visibility 2px Teal ring (`#0D9488`).
- **Secondary (Action/Liquidity):** Solid `#0D9488` with white text. Reserved for primary conversion events like "Claim Invoice", "Apply for Collateral-Free Loan", or "Reconcile ITC".
- **Outline / Ghost:** Transparent background with `1px solid #CBD5E1` border and `#0F2942` text. Used for secondary administrative workflows ("Export GSTR", "Download E-Way Bill").

### Status Pills
- Compact, pill-shaped tags (`font-weight: 600`, `font-size: 11px`, `letter-spacing: 0.03em`) featuring contextual icon indicators:
  - **Paid:** Green background (`#ECFDF5`), deep emerald text (`#047857`), dot checkmark.
  - **Pending:** Amber background (`#FFFBEB`), amber text (`#B45309`), hollow clock indicator.
  - **Partially Paid:** Blue tint (`#EFF6FF`), cobalt text (`#1D4ED8`), dual-tone split circle.
  - **Overdue:** Crimson wash (`#FEF2F2`), ruby text (`#B91C1C`), caution marker with days overdue appended (e.g., `Overdue (45+ Days)`).

### Metric Cards
- Designed for rapid cashflow diagnostics. Contains:
  - Upper row: Metric label in uppercase muted text (`label-sm`), right-aligned informational tooltip or trend percentage badge.
  - Core: Bold currency figure in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with prominent `₹` symbol.
  - Lower row: Sub-descriptor showing statutory cutoff dates or invoice counts.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Default: Height 44px (48px on mobile), `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Focus state: Border shifts to `#0F2942` accompanied by a 3px soft Teal glow (`rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)`).
- Specific Form Controls: Monospaced uppercase styling for GSTIN, PAN, and IFSC entry, complete with auto-formatting blocks.

### Tables & Data Grids
- Clean borders without heavy vertical grid lines. Striped row highlights are omitted in favor of clear hover states (`#F1F5F9`).
- Numeric financial columns align right with monospace tabular figures (`Inter`). In-cell quick-actions emerge on hover for desktop and persist via ellipsis action buttons on mobile.
